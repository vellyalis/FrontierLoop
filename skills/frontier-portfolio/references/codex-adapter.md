# Codex adapter

Keep Codex mechanics separate from the general portfolio policy.

## Place instructions at the narrowest durable scope

- User-level Plugin/Skill: reusable cross-project workflow. FrontierLoop does not edit global `AGENTS.md`.
- Project `AGENTS.md`: product value, ownership, platform limits, and real
  acceptance surface.
- Current request: the immediate outcome, constraints, correction, and temporary
  context.
- Skill: the reusable two-workstream method.
- Interaction profile: presentation and steering preferences only.

Do not copy the full skill into an `AGENTS.md` file.

## Long-running work

Use an unattended goal only after outcome, boundaries, evaluator, and stop point
are clear enough to survive context compaction. Otherwise advance to the next
judgeable artifact or human-only decision boundary.

Persist a compact resume packet when another task may continue the work. The
current artifact, observed difference, rejected premise, pending decision, and
next move are usually enough; raw conversation is not.

## Delegation and independent tasks

Delegate only independent work with an observable return contract. Keep product
direction, portfolio selection, and integration with the owning agent.

Use separate Codex tasks when long-lived context isolation or experimental
independence is itself required. Do not use a subagent merely to imitate a second
idea while sharing the same premise.

Fresh review is conditional. Use it for high consequence, difficult rollback,
uncertain root cause, or repeated failure. Give a reviewer the observable goal,
complete diff or exact base and head, constraints, and evidence; withhold the
implementation reasoning and preferred verdict.

## Controlled evaluation

For a benchmark, isolate workspaces, pin the model and effort, fingerprint the
instruction surface, and hide arm identity, expected route, private checks, and
sibling results. Freeze artifacts and judgments before revealing the mapping.

`codex exec --ignore-user-config` belongs to controlled evaluation, not normal
product work.
